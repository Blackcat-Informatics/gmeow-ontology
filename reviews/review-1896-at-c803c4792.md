## Answers to the brief

**1. Framework.** `logic:instantiatesFramework` names "WHICH reasoning machinery a term belongs to"
(logic/module.ttl:1033). The two sentences the coder cited exist:

- 1033: "absence is meaningful — a plain domain term that traffics in no special discipline carries no
  framework".
- 2487: "Absence is meaningful — a plain domain term that traffics in no special discipline carries no
  framework, and none is asserted".

`logic:confidence` (2908-2912) declares no framework. `logic:evidenceStrength` (3054) and
`logic:probability` (3414) declare theirs. `logic:EpistemicFramework` (1083) is the machinery of
doxastic accessibility, knowing-that, evidential warrant (`logic:evidenceStrength`) and argumentation.
It does not cover confidence. `logic:ProbabilisticFramework` (1090) calls `logic:confidence`
"epistemic metadata".

LOGIC-SEMANTICS.md 116-124 and 304-307 treat confidence as evidence-only metadata. Confidence does not
place a claim in a context, and its fact keeps marginal 1.0. The domain terms that do declare
EpistemicFramework are all relators that take part in justification: Attestation, Argument and
Observation.

The right answer is no framework, which is what the canonical owner declares. The EpistemicFramework
declaration is REQUIRED-1.

The new text is otherwise true and consistent with `logic:confidence`. The example
`ex:alice ex:knows ex:bob {| gmeow:confidence 0.8 |} .` is valid RDF 1.2 Turtle annotation syntax.

**2. Grounding form.** The typing claim holds:

- `gmeow:confidence` is a `logic:AnnotationProperty` (provenance/module.ttl:178).
- `logic:confidence` is a `logic:DatatypeProperty` (logic/module.ttl:2909).

The precedent exists (observations:747, standpoint:126), but it relates two domain properties to each
other. It is not a domain carrier relating to its `logic:` surface. For that relation the repository
has a machine-readable edge, `logic:formalizes` (logic/module.ttl:18111). It is "a bridge annotation
from a logic: foundational term to the gmeow: domain concept it formalizes", is authored in the
grounding slice, and is "an annotation property, never a reasoned axiom, so it carries no DL or EL
profile weight". Live uses include `logic:ArgumentationSemantics logic:formalizes gmeow:Argument …`
(logic:112).

The DL-downcast argument therefore excludes only `rdfs:subPropertyOf`. It does not exclude the
sanctioned edge. Leaving the link as prose leaves the semantic link missing, which is what the issue
asks the branch to tell apart from inherited grounding. That is REQUIRED-2.
