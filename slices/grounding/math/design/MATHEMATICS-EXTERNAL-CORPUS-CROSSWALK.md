# External mathematics corpus crosswalk

## Audit boundary

This ledger audits the 95 topic records in the private comparison snapshot supplied for this
coverage uplift against GMEOW commit `fe7244a5bf6a202bbfb68a3b9212f31ac53e91dd`. The source project is deliberately unnamed and is
not a dependency of the shipped ontology; only the general mathematical coverage decisions below
are public. A later snapshot or ontology change requires a new audit rather than silently changing
this one.

The topic slug is an audit key, not an ontology IRI. A mathematical structure is owned by `math:`;
formula, correspondence, query, and reasoning contracts are owned by `logic:`; signs and compact
notation are owned by `lang:` and GMN; physical storage and query execution remain PurRDF concerns.
Semantic readings of vectors, topology, dynamics, or numerical scores are always framed
`gmeow:Observation` or `math:ResidualInterpretationClaim` records with a `gmeow:vantage` and evidence.
They are never intrinsic properties of coordinates. This applies Principles 1, 4, 5, 11, 12, 17,
and 19.

## Disposition policy

- **REUSE** means one already-shipped GMEOW identity or contract has the topic's reusable meaning.
  Consumers use that identity directly and no synonym is minted.
- **COMPOSE** means the reusable meaning is completely expressible as a stated composition of
  existing or accepted-in-this-audit identities, relations, and claim framing. No umbrella synonym
  is minted merely to name the composition.
- **EXTEND** means an existing canonical family is the correct owner, but the topic identifies a
  reusable specialization or structural role that the family lacks. The named target is added under
  that family with the same annotation, logic, conformance, translation, and GMN obligations.
- **MINT** means the topic denotes a reusable mathematical identity absent from GMEOW and not merely
  a configuration of a broader identity. The named `math:` target becomes the canonical identity;
  corpus interpretations remain separate observations.
- **PROFILE** means the topic is a corpus, product, storage, security, workflow, algorithm-selection,
  or interpretive policy. It is represented downstream as a deterministic `gmeow:Profile`, pipeline
  selection, or evidence-bearing observation over the stated GMEOW terms. It does not create a new
  `math:` term. Explicit profile selection is not capability degradation: once selected, every
  declared input, output, check, and preservation claim is mandatory.

For `MINT` and `EXTEND`, the target names below are the public GMEOW identities. For `COMPOSE`, the
listed expression is the complete canonical construction. For `PROFILE`, the listed owners and
descriptor terms are the profile's exact semantic dependencies; the consumer may name its profile
locally without exporting that local name into GMEOW.

## Cross-cutting acceptance

Every new `math:` target above is canonical in the grounding slice, carries complete labels,
definitions, ownership, FR/ZH translations, logic typing, competency and counterexample coverage, and
an explicit GMN-1 symbol-candidate disposition. External links are `logic:Correspondence` records with
honest relation, direction, law rung, evidence, and preservation; a same-name or nearby concept never
earns equivalence by convenience.

Every `PROFILE` row is intentionally downstream. It may select an explicit DAG path, carrier,
algorithm, security view, or output, but the selection must be deterministic and cache-keyed. Missing
capability hard-fails. No profile may silently drop evidence, use a weaker implementation, turn a
geometric score into truth, or move storage semantics into `math:`. This is the no-optionality
doctrine applied to the crosswalk rather than a ban on explicit feature selection.
