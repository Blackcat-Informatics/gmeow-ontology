# Story-building records

The story-building properties describe a consumer's attributed records. Existing
`InformationObject`, `Collection`, `CreativeWork`, `ContentSegment`, narrative
position and provenance terms carry their meaning; no new story identity or
universal plot structure is introduced (Principles 4, 9 and 17).

Acceptance belongs to a person, not to the generator that proposed a candidate.
The asserted-link law follows exact lowered statement subject, predicate and
object components to that person's attribution. A separate reifier-guarded law
also checks quoted acceptances whose contents were never asserted. These laws
require completed statement-metadata lowering. They do not broaden its supported
fragment, authenticate the person or make a quoted proposition true.

The gathering and structuring links are explicit completion reports. Each named
activity carries the established `logic:Closed` boundary. Structuring requires a
gathered stage on that same story, rather than accepting some other story's
completed gathering. No timestamp or member is invented. Producers write markers
last; source integrity cannot certify transaction order or enumerate members
missing from both the supplied scope and the stored story. Empty selections are
not forbidden by a fabricated minimum-member rule. `artifactComplete` is a
separate whole-artifact claim, and generation provenance is stated only when an
activity actually produced a resource.

Curated membership uses the existing collection `hasPart` spine. Structural
segments use `hasSegment`; events and their frame-bound positions, narration
seams and citations remain independently represented. The offered reading scope
is evidence about inputs, not a claim that every offered record was selected.
These distinctions retain the actual source identities without inferring a
preferred story from a shared record set (Principles 9 and 10).

Focus and recollection wording use the prose datatype union, preserving plain
strings when language was not stated and tagged/directional literals when it
was. Neither field is functional. The cue is a possible recall handle, not a
matcher execution receipt or a preferred event label.

Generic repair and intention-witness relations belong in agency records, which
already depends on observations, teleology and provenance. Narrative consumes
that core owner; no core slice imports this extension. A reported fix may be a
repair activity or an artifact, which is why the range is their honest union.
The observation-purpose link is distinct from observed feature, motivation and
goal satisfaction. Neither relation proves an execution or outcome merely by
being present (Principles 4, 7, 9, 10 and 17).
