# Issue #2684: vocabulary: a frame relation between standpoints that does not narrow

State: OPEN   Repo: Blackcat-Informatics/gmeow-ontology   Forge: github
Labels: enhancement, foundational, logic, terms

## Body

Request: a frame relation between a standpoint and the context its readings
were made within, which does not narrow the standpoint.

Need: a reading of a work is made from some frame. Two standpoints may
share a context (the readings were made within that context) without one
being a sharper case of the other. Today sharpening is the only relation
between standpoints, and using it for a shared context would wrongly make
one frame a refinement of the other.

Proposed term: madeWithinContextOf, relating a standpoint that exists for
reading works to the frame whose context its readings were made within.

Explicitly not a sharpening: the subject does not become a case of the
object. It is for standpoints that exist for reading works, not for a
person who holds claims about other things as well.
