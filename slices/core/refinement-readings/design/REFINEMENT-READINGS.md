# Scoped relational findings and reading limits

`FirstOccurrence` and `RelationOccasion` inherit their actual ontological sort
through `Refinement` and `Observation`. They reuse source identity,
versioned-process vantage and outcome rather than introducing a parallel
reading engine. Milestone and limit aggregates inherit `InformationObject` and
remain attributed annotations. Shared relation and scope properties have no
global domain that would turn those annotations into observations.

A `ReadingScope` records the exact offered inputs. Source chronology references
an existing `TemporalFrame` through a true subproperty of `hasReferenceFrame`;
it does not misuse transaction concurrency protocols
or turn coarse dates into timestamp instants. Original source assertion time,
refiner observation time and record transaction time remain distinct.

The mathematical set and membership APIs were inspected. `math:hasElement`
declares mathematical objects as its members, so arbitrary source records are
not forced into that sort merely to give their input enumeration a set label.
The scope enumeration is operational provenance, not a new mathematical set
calculus. Likewise, a language-bearing `findingSummary` is text, not an invented
lexeme, lexical sense or analyzed `lang:SurfaceForm`. Native tagged and directional
literals preserve language without making an unsupported linguistic analysis.

`ReadingLimit` is a SubKind of the existing `QueryLossLedger` and uses its
canonical `ledgerLoss` membership directly. It does not specialize `QueryLoss`:
a zero-count aggregate must not fabricate an unavailable input.
Its reason vocabulary genuinely specializes `QueryLossCause`; actual members
reuse `lossConcerns`, `lossCause` and the existing ledger identity. The selected
refiner can report inability to correlate particular RDF terms without making
that limitation a property of RDF 1.2.

Canonical domains, ranges, local value restrictions and required paths supply
the structural grounding. Local single-value restrictions apply only to fields
that define one relational reading or aggregate group; there are no new global
functional declarations. Multiple attributed reports remain separate records.
Eight realized integrity formulas enforce resource-or-name presence, actual IRI
bindings, source scope membership, loss scope/cause coherence, empty zero counts
and retained evidence for positive counts. They use targetable outer universal
guards and supported native term predicates (Principle 17).

Earliest-in-source status and the exact distinct-record count are attributed
consumer computations. No formula claims to execute source relation selection,
repair missing assertion times, certify global absence or verify count arithmetic
beyond the explicit intrinsic checks. No parser, ordering executor or statistical
framework is added.

External grounding belongs to the logic, language and mathematics owners under
[the grounding contract](../../../../docs/GROUNDING.md). Existing upper sorts and
provenance/observation relation owners supply the applicable bridges. Scoped
first-occurrence claims and zero-capable limit aggregates have no justified
exact external equivalence from this source review; no downstream duplicate
alignment or unsupported preservation claim is authored (Principles 5 and 19).

## Stateful readings and temporal ownership

The three additional reading classes specialize the existing `Refinement`
observation, inheriting its source and process-vantage interpretation. They share
`ReadingScope` rather than inventing a second input-set model. The new evidence
relations are genuine `wasDerivedFrom` specializations: they retain the original
settling record, counted record or counted act, and do not declare that a
world-state was changed by a reading.

Open-item state is a local, single-valued report. Separate process readings can
disagree without forcing their states to merge. The existing coreference
`supersedes` relation records a successor's replacement of a prior reading while
both remain accessible; its actual domain and range admit observation records.
Nothing adds a global status or retracts source facts. Seeded settled and
unsettled states have the two intrinsic evidence laws described in the docs.

Five further scope laws retain the source and all explicitly supplied evidence
within offered records. Together the seven additional formulas have directly
targetable guards: a class atom, or conjunctions with source/scope atoms. Any
disjunction occurs within such a guard or in a consequent, never as an unsupported
OR-only outer guard. No generic predicate is broadened into a mandatory state
field for unrelated observations or ledgers.

The counted habit members are actual `Activity` occurrences, whose existing
owner makes them events. An activity designation is deliberately `logic:Thing`:
a source may name an activity description rather than one particular event, and
this reading supplies no basis for forcing it into a narrower external sort.
The two reported counts lack a universal denominator contract, so a chosen-count
inequality or a member-count equality would be an invented invariant.

`DayPart` and its four initial values belong to the temporal owner and remain
open. They are quality classifications, not instants or fixed-hour intervals.
The consuming figure binds holder, day part and temporal frame together. Its
specific frame relation inherits `hasReferenceFrame` without casting a temporal
value as an observation or making the process vantage the holder by default.
The frame and holder supply the context for interpreting the classification;
the vocabulary does not claim an unimplemented boundary calculator.

The canonical observation, provenance, coreference and temporal owners provide
the meaningful grounding. No exact external correspondence is justified for a
scoped absence reading, a reported segmentation or an unspecified denominator;
unsupported equivalences would erase these distinctions (Principles 4, 5, 11,
17 and 19).

## Empty-computation evidence

The new source-attribution limit reuses QueryLossCause ancestry, scoped loss
identity and the existing count/evidence obligations. It introduces no weaker
input-selection branch and no assertion of originality from missing provenance.

The foundNothing field selects an explicit refinement report. Guarded formulas
require its own scope, declared vantage, generating activity and explicit unresolved
outcome while excluding selected results. They do not manufacture completion
from absence. Conditional field uniqueness and report identity apply only to
that selected profile, retaining independent executions and standpoints.

Two-way scope/provenance laws make wasDerivedFrom precisely the report's
declared input roster with its exact RDF identities. The same generating
activity retains an actual used-input claim to that scope information object,
without imposing the used relation's Entity range on arbitrary source terms.
Existing COUNT DISTINCT over the direct provenance path can therefore
compare with foundNothing without adding a cross-node counting primitive.
The separate zero/positive presence law covers empty aggregate groups. Input
records are counted by RDF identity, so repeated visits do not inflate the
declared batch.

The source checker can establish these structural bindings and exact count
agreement. It cannot authenticate that the selected computation ran or read
every declared input. Those remain explicit producer claims, not silent
fallbacks or a global absence conclusion. No reader, parser, executor, new
datatype engine or test framework is added.

## Being-focused contact readings

ContactReading deepens the existing Refinement observation for a being the
holder can address. The actual feature is that being; actual source records
remain provenance through wasDerivedFrom and any supplied ReadingScope, not a
second observedFeature. The actual named/versioned observer is the vantage,
distinct from the holder of the records. This uses the existing single-feature
invariant and does not weaken any feature/vantage obligation. Source-focused
FirstOccurrence, RelationOccasion and the existing reading profiles retain their
record-as-feature interpretation. The general Refinement prose admits both
source-focused and referent-focused evidence-derived readings.

The reading-local contactName is a supplied name selection within actual
records and chronology. The flat text is not a name-bearing property on the
being or a fullName specialization, and no Appellation type is inferred for
the reading. Earlier names remain source-backed aliases. This local selection
does not privilege a global name, erase history, resolve missing chronology,
invent language or infer identity from a matching string. Complete supplied
literals retain datatype, lexical form, language and base direction.

ContactKind and ContactRingOutcome are open QualityValue vocabularies with
individual values. Their roles, and contactName, are optional and nonfunctional;
property-global closure checks only supplied endpoints. No closed enumeration,
global functional property, local maximum, mandatory name/kind/outcome, new
Formula or ring-classification engine is selected. The inner-agent value reports
the supplied environment classification. The correspondent value establishes
only the reading's message-attribution classification, with no automatic
Person/Agent or authenticated-author inference. Independent classifications
retain their own attributed evidence.

Ring decision status is separate from both actual ring identity and the overall
refinement outcome. Stated reports the holder's rule-based decision; undecided
reports that the stated rules choose no ring, with none stated or guessed by
the reading. Absence is not undecided. No unspecified ring-bearing predicate is
invented merely to write a prohibition Formula, and no new holder role is added.
The vocabulary does not certify that a reading or its rules ran. All three roles
use existing generic literal/class endpoint projection; no runtime or dependency
change is needed (Principles 4, 9, 10, 17 and 19).

## Framed presence and offered counts

The presence and offered roles retain distinct source-reported counts of
situations. They use the sole canonical `math:Quantity` rather than parallel
scalar fields or a new mathematical carrier. Their source values remain exact
`xsd:nonNegativeInteger` literals, with valid finite numeric interpretation,
numeric-value equality for any retained lexical aliases, the dimensionless
dimension and one actual complete counting frame (Principles 4, 11 and 17).

The figure’s existing holder, activity designation, DayPart, TemporalFrame and
source scope remain distinct from the counted population and its declared
grouping. `habitMember` retains actual Activity evidence; its membership does
not define a situation denominator. Offered counts also retain the guidance in
force. No choice/presence/offered inequality, member-count equality, preference
judgment or source-subject assertion follows from these reported values.

Two global supplied-value closures carry actual HabitFigure and Quantity
endpoint obligations. Direct figure-local allValuesFrom restrictions and
qualified maximum-one bounds admit at most one quantity per supplied role.
There is no class-local presence closure, global functionality or requirement
to supply either role. The grouped Formula’s outer class guard selects actual
HabitFigures; sixteen independent role-guarded arms bind their own variables
and require magnitudes, dimensions and frames without positive joined
existentials. Original literal identity remains separate from numeric equality.

The existing Math, Places and logic owners supply the actual carrier, complete
frame profile, numeric admission and validation projection. The counting frame’s
source-supported meaning remains an evidence obligation; endpoint and value
checks do not certify population selection, grouping, source truth, guidance
history or execution. A temporal civil frame is not silently substituted for a
counting interpretation. No counter engine, rule/window parameter, boundary
calculator or new external correspondence is implied (Principles 5 and 19).
