    GMEOW — Global Metadata and Entity Ontology for the Web¶

    Version: 0.1.0
    Namespace: https://blackcatinformatics.ca/gmeow/
    Terms: 579 classes, 1328 properties, 4929 individuals, 1 datatypes

    A reasoning-centric, OWL 2 DL, upper-ontology-grounded super-vocabulary that unifies document metadata, entity descriptions, legal agreements, contacts and person-centric data. GMEOW is grounded in gUFO and aligned to FOAF, REL, DOAP, PROV-O, schema.org and Wikidata.
