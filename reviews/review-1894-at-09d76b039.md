### 2. Journal cells (R2)

**Projection** (`aggregate_balance_select`, shapes.rs 1824-1830). An inner SELECT per
`$this, ?group`:

- `$this journalEntryPostings ?posting`

- `BIND(IF(?direction = debit, ?val, 0))`, and the same for credit
- `SUM` of each, `GROUP BY $this ?group`

The outer query keeps rows where `?sumDebits != ?sumCredits`.

| Case | Groups | Rows | Result |
|---|---|---|---|

- **Shape name.** `procedural_shape_iri(gmeow:JournalEntryBalanceConstraint)` is
  `https://blackcatinformatics.ca/gmeow/` + `JournalEntryBalanceConstraint` +
  `ProceduralConstraintShape`. That equals the pin.
- **Soleness counts shapes, not rows.** Every Violation result must satisfy
  `matches_source(source_shape, pin)` (exec.rs 1070-1086). Rows and results are not counted, and
  the design comment at 1012-1033 states the per-shape unit. So the split case is sole whether or
  not the engine collapses its two identical results; `dedupe_validation_results` (exec.rs 853)
  merges them if they are identical. The coder's reading is correct.
- **No other law fires** in either cell. The postings count is still 2. Each new amount node is a
  typed `MonetaryAmount`, which satisfies the posting's class check. Nothing else reads amount
  values.
- **A posting amount with no value or no currency** drops out of the inner join. That is a
  property of the constraint, not a gap against the issue. Presence is owned elsewhere: the
  amount owner's closure requires `math:quantityValue`, and `gmeow:requiresFrame` on
